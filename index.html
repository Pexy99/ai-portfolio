Project 1 · Core Challenge

Evaluating Models on Real Notices

↓
Requirement extraction failedZero requirements from two real attachments
↓
gpt-4.1-miniExtracted 7 and 19 items from the same documents
↓
Decision criteria · examples · rule validationCorrected 3 location-condition errors

Problem

The low-cost model missed explicit eligibility conditions in two real notices, requiring a trade-off between extraction quality and cost.

Solution

Compared models on identical documents and prioritized coverage of mandatory requirements over incremental cost.

01Extraction Quality vs. Cost

Compared nano and mini on six attachments. Mini cost 4× more per input token, but projected usage was only $1–2 per month.

02Decision Criteria and Positive/Negative Examples

Defined location-restriction criteria, added positive and negative examples, and revalidated outputs with rules.

Results

  • Extracted conditions from the same notices: 0 → 7 and 0 → 19
  • Empty forms remained at zero extractions; corrected 3 of 8 location errors

Problem

Evidence Selection: The system needed risk-specific evidence with links to original sources.

Data Boundary: Raw credentials could enter the corpus and LLM context.

Approach

Encoded retrieval scope and provenance in metadata instead of relying on semantic similarity alone.

01Curate and Normalize Evidence

Tagged evidence by risk, system, and action before normalizing it to a shared schema.

02Validate Provenance and Sensitive Data

Tracked provenance and changes while blocking raw credentials and duplicates before ingestion.

Results

  • Built the data contract, DDL, pipeline, and 9 evidence chunks
  • Enabled metadata-filtered retrieval and answer provenance
  • Designed 7 remediation evaluation scenarios with expected evidence
